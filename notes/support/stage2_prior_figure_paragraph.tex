We next investigate the choice of prior for stage-2 matching.
For each perturbed ensemble member, stage 1 uses the perturbed prior.
We compare two variants:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item Stage 2 uses the original, unperturbed prior.
\end{enumerate}
Final candidate selection uses the original X/Z DEM log odds in both cases.
